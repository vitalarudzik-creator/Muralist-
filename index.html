FAQ

Common questions

Didn't find your answer? Ask on Telegram, I reply quickly.

How much does a mural cost?

From $30 per m² for graphics to $80 per m² for realism. Outdoor work costs 20% more because of facade paint and varnish. The minimum order is $300. The calculator above gives you an estimate.

Can I see what it will look like before I pay?

Yes. I draw the sketch right on a photo of your wall. You review it, ask for changes, and only then we sign the contract.

How long does an outdoor mural last?

8–12 years with proper preparation. I prime the wall, paint with facade acrylics and finish with a UV and anti-graffiti varnish. Outdoor work comes with a 5-year warranty.

Do I need a permit to paint a facade?

Apartment buildings need consent from the building association or management company. Historic buildings need approval from the heritage authority. I help prepare the sketch and documents for approval.

Will there be smell or mess indoors?

I use water-based acrylics with no strong smell. I cover floors and furniture with film and clean up afterwards. In offices I can work evenings and weekends.

Do you work outside Kyiv?

Yes, anywhere in Ukraine and abroad. Travel within Kyiv and the region is free. For other cities, travel and accommodation are paid separately.
